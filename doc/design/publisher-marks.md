# Publisher artwork

[Documentation index](../README.md)

Used to identify the original news outlet, with its name alongside the image.
These are publisher trademarks, not Worldline branding.

- [`cna.svg`](../../public/publishers/cna.svg): official transparent white variant from https://www.channelnewsasia.com/sites/default/themes/mc_cna_theme/images/logo-white.svg
- [`bbc.svg`](../../public/publishers/bbc.svg): official BBC logo paths from https://www.bbc.com, presented in white on the dark interface. Shape and proportions retained.

Retrieved 26 October 2026. Other publishers use their site favicon through the bounded same-origin logo endpoint. The surrounding badge is transparent; an unavailable mark uses a generic newspaper icon.
